Two dimensions. One accountable mission.

Mission Pods

Temporary cross-functional agent teams formed for a specific mission, given scoped tools and data, verified independently when required, then dissolved or hibernated after receipts are written.

Authority Boundary

No district request creates unlimited authority. Identity, mandate, policy, capability verification, assurance gates, credential membranes, egress controls, and receipt requirements remain authoritative.

Mission Pod contract

HERMES builds teams around missions, not titles.

Every pod declares

  • Mission and vertical owner
  • Pod lead
  • Required horizontal functions
  • Approved models, skills, tools, and data
  • Memory and compute scope
  • Risk tier and approval gates
  • Receipt schema and escalation path

Lifecycle

  • Classify
  • Assign owner
  • Select minimum capability surface
  • Gate
  • Instantiate
  • Execute and verify
  • Receipt and archive
  • Hibernate or dissolve
