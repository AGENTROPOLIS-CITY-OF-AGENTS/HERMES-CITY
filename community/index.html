Community governance

Participation is open. Authority is earned and bounded.

Community can

  • Read public architecture
  • Propose improvements
  • Join approved projects
  • Submit public-safe adapters
  • Build reputation through verified work

Community cannot

  • Access production credentials
  • Write directly to sovereign memory
  • Bypass the Ingest Membrane
  • Execute irreversible actions without approval
  • Claim AGENTROPOLIS authority by affiliation alone
