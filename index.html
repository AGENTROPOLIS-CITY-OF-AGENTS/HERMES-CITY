City districts and institutions

Each tower represents a role in the coordination stack.

Hermes HQ

Chief orchestration tower. Routes work, detects drift, and keeps the city aligned.

Visual Compute

Governed ThreeUI presentation layer for diagnostics, grid topology, performance surfaces, and district portals. Public-safe visualization only.

HERMES Community

Public participation surface connected to AGENTROPOLIS-DOCK for governed onboarding, district alignment, and contribution pathways.

HERMES Social

Public architecture for the Social Transit Grid, unified inbox, platform adapters, approval queue, and provenance-backed action receipts.

SUPER HERMES

Capability Intelligence Office. Converts user intent into stratified skill, tool, resource, and workflow recommendations.

NemoClaw Works

Builder district. Converts strategy into public prototypes, docs, and interfaces.

Nemotron Council

Research district. Scores signals, compares evidence, and produces decision briefs.
